## COPY-READY POST

AI didn't make my team faster at writing code. It made us slower at shipping bad code.

Over the last year we moved AI into the real workflow at AKIJ iBOS. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and MCP servers wired into our own internal tools. The hype says the AI writes the code for you. Here is what actually changed.

- The first draft comes faster. That was never the bottleneck.
- Review got sharper, not lighter. The AI flags the boring mistakes so the humans can argue about the real ones.
- The expensive bugs still come from not understanding the problem. AI doesn't fix that. It just gets you to the misunderstanding faster.

So we treat it like any other power tool. It makes a good engineer faster, and a careless one dangerous.

The job didn't change. Ship work that stays shipped: tested, monitored, and understandable to whoever maintains it next.

AI just moved where the thinking happens.
