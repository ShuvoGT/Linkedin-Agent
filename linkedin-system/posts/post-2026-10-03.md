## COPY-READY POST

I built a dashboard nobody opened.

It had every chart. Meta Ads, Google Ads, Analytics, Play Console, and our CRM, all in one place at AKIJ iBOS. We called it Marketing OS. And people still pinged me for numbers in Slack.

The problem wasn't the data. It was the one extra step: open the tab.

So I flipped it. Instead of waiting for people to come to the dashboard, the dashboard comes to them, one email every morning with the numbers that moved and a recommended next step.

Usage didn't go up. It stopped mattering. The report just shows up.

The lesson stuck with me.

- A tool people have to remember to open is a tool people forget.
- For anything daily, push beats pull.
- The best internal tool removes a decision, not just a click.

Build for where attention already is. Then it stays shipped.
