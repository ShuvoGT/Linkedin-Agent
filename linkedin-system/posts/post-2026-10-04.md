## COPY-READY POST

You don't need to leave Dhaka to build for the world.

Over six years I've shipped 300+ websites and apps for clients in Bangladesh, the US, the UK and Australia. All of it from here. No relocation, no visa, just good work delivered on time.

The internet flattened the map a long time ago. What's left is the part nobody can outsource:

- Writing code someone else can actually maintain.
- Communicating clearly across time zones.
- Owning the outcome, not just the ticket.

To the junior devs messaging me from Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet: tomar skill-i tomar passport. The address on your CV matters less every year. Your commit history matters more.

Build in public. Help the person one step behind you. The community grows when we stop treating knowledge like a secret.

Same standard, wherever you sit: ship work that stays shipped.
